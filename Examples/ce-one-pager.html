INTERNAL · OPERATING REVIEW

SECOND QUARTER REVIEW

COLLECTIVE EDGE · APEX · ROYAL

Operating committee, closed session

SHARED SERVICES

Both operating companies grew on one back office.

Apex Paramedics and Royal Ambulance closed the quarter on one finance team, one dispatch platform and one credentialing pipeline. Run volume rose at both. Shared services headcount did not move.

QUARTER IN REVIEW

Shared services held.

Billing runs on one connection. Both companies clear through a single payer gateway. Days sales outstanding closed at 41 days for Apex and 38 days for Royal, against 54 and 47 a year ago.

Credentialing is the constraint. A new medic waits 23 days between signed offer and first shift. Nineteen of those days sit with county accreditation, which no shared service shortens.

Dispatch consolidated in May. One platform now carries both companies. The committee recorded the remaining exposure as “one outage, two companies dark,” and moved failover design to the September agenda.

TWO BOOKS

Where the two companies diverge.

Fleet age. Apex runs a median vehicle age of 3.1 years against Royal’s 6.4. Royal’s replacement schedule carries fourteen ambulances across 2026–2028 and is the larger call on group capital.

Payer mix. Royal bills 61% commercial. Apex bills 68% government. The two books do not move together, so a bad rate year at one company does not compound at the other.
