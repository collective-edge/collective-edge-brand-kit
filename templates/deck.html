Three handoff forms became one, and crews got five minutes back.

The form asks for four things.

  • Status of the unit, with fuel and mileage.
  • Open maintenance items.
  • Controlled substance count.
  • Notes for the next crew.

  • Completed at the unit, before the crew leaves.
