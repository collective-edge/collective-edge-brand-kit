WHERE THE TIME GOES

What the quarter shows.

Volume moved late. Sixty-one percent of runs now originate between 11:00 and 16:00. Crews staged at the three highest-volume campuses absorbed the shift without an added vehicle.

Wait time is the constraint. Median call to wheels held at 18 min while bed-hold minutes at receiving facilities rose. Fleet size is not the limit.

Payer approval drives downtime. A run held for approval averages 34 min of idle vehicle time. The pre-approval queue closes that gap when the referring case manager files before the discharge order posts.
