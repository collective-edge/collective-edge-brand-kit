Operations report

One handoff form is ready for both companies.

Apex Paramedics and Royal Ambulance tested a single one-page handoff form for six weeks. Handoff time fell at both. The form goes live on 1 December.

For

Operations leads

From

Collective Edge Operations

Period

1 Oct to 11 Nov 2026

Summary.

Crews at four stations used the one-page form in place of the three versions in use today. Each crew completed the form at the unit before leaving the station, and each supervisor signed once per shift.

Median handoff time fell by five minutes at Apex Paramedics and by four minutes at Royal Ambulance. No station reported a missed maintenance item during the test.

What crews reported.

Crews asked for one change: a larger notes field. The published form carries it. Supervisors reported that one signature per shift removed a queue at shift change.

Risks.

The form depends on a tablet template. Eleven units do not have it loaded. Systems loads the template on every unit by 26 November.

A unit without a loaded tablet uses the printed form. The supervisor scans it at the end of the shift.

Next steps.

  1. 1Station leads brief every supervisor by 24 November.
  2. 2Systems loads the tablet template on every unit by 26 November.
  3. 3All crews hand off on the new form from the first shift on 1 December.
  4. 4Operations reviews handoff time again on 15 January 2027.

Method.

Handoff time is the interval between the outgoing crew opening the form and the supervisor signing it. Both times come from the tablet log. A handoff on the printed form is timed by the supervisor and entered at the end of the shift.

The four stations were chosen to cover both companies, day and night shifts, and the two busiest posts. Each station ran the three forms for two weeks, then the one form for six.

Limits.

Six weeks is one schedule rotation. The January review covers a second rotation and the holiday period.
