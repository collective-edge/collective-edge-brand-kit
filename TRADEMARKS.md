# Collective Edge · names and logos

The names, logos and brand colors in this repository belong to Collective Edge. That covers the Collective Edge name, the `CE │ COLLECTIVE EDGE` lockup, the block mark and the phrase “Powered by Collective Edge”.

This repository is public so that the people who make Collective Edge material can reach the files. Public does not mean free to use. The files are here to be used for Collective Edge, with its knowledge.

## You may, without asking

- Use the logos on work that Collective Edge asked you to make: printing, signage, a website, a document, a deck.
- Show the logo, unaltered, to name Collective Edge as a partner, customer, employer or vendor, where that is true and Collective Edge knows about it.
- Use the name in plain text to refer to the company: news, reviews, comparisons, job histories.
- Read the standard and learn from it: the layout rules, the type system and the checks.

## Ask first

- Any use of a logo on something you sell, or in advertising.
- Any use that suggests Collective Edge sponsors, endorses or is part of something it did not agree to.
- A co-branded piece that Collective Edge has not seen.
- Any use on vehicles, uniforms, patches or badges.

## Never

- Redraw, recolor, stretch, crop or add to a logo. Use the files as they ship.
- Use the name or a logo in a company name, product name, domain or social handle.
- Use the name, a logo or the palette in a way that could pass your work off as coming from Collective Edge.
- Present yourself as Collective Edge or as its staff.

## How to ask

Open an issue in this repository, or ask your contact at Collective Edge. Say what you want to make, where it will appear and for how long. Permission is given case by case and in writing. No answer is not a yes.

Collective Edge may change these terms. The version on `main` is the one in force.
